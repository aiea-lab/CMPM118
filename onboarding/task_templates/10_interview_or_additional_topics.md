# Task 10: Interview or Additional Topics

## Objective

Synthesize what you learned through a research interview or a focused additional topic.

## Prerequisites and resources

[PREREQUISITE TASKS OR KNOWLEDGE]. Use [RESOURCE LINKS] selected by the project lead.

## Tasks

- [ ] Agree with [PROJECT LEAD] on one route: interview or additional topic.
- [ ] For the interview route, prepare questions grounded in Tasks 3–9, interview [PROJECT MEMBER OR RESEARCHER], and take notes with their permission.
- [ ] For the interview route, summarize insights about methods, limitations, and open questions, distinguishing the interviewee’s statements from your interpretation.
- [ ] For the additional-topic route, study [ADDITIONAL TOPIC] using [RESOURCE] and complete [FOCUSED EXERCISE OR EXPERIMENT].
- [ ] For either route, connect the findings to earlier tasks and propose one scoped research question and next step.

## Deliverable

For an interview, submit the prepared questions, a one-to-two-page synthesis, and a proposed next step. For an additional topic, submit a one-to-two-page technical summary, exercise or experiment evidence, and a proposed next step. No recording is required. Submit to Canvas under Deliverable 10, or [SUBMISSION LOCATION] if not enrolled.

## Evaluation criteria

The submission must demonstrate the objective, include the requested evidence, and explain results and limitations accurately. Code must include enough instructions to reproduce the example or experiment.
