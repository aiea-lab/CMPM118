# Task 9: Paper Reading and Annotation

## Objective

Critically read a project paper and connect its claims to the methods and experiments from Tasks 3–8.

## Prerequisites and resources

Complete Tasks 3–8. Read [PAPER TITLE AND LINK] selected by [PROJECT LEAD].

## Tasks

- [ ] Annotate the research question, motivation, central claim, method, data, evaluation, and limitations.
- [ ] Define unfamiliar terms and identify assumptions required by the method.
- [ ] Trace one figure or table from experimental setup to the authors’ conclusion.
- [ ] Identify one well-supported claim and one that needs more evidence.
- [ ] Connect the paper to the baseline and extension from Tasks 7 and 8.
- [ ] Propose one follow-up experiment and prepare two discussion questions.

## Deliverable

Submit the annotated paper (PDF comments or a shared annotation link) and a one-page synthesis to Canvas under Deliverable 9, or [SUBMISSION LOCATION] if not enrolled. Cite page, section, figure, or table locations for major observations and distinguish the authors’ claims from your interpretation.

## Evaluation criteria

Annotations must cover the listed elements. The synthesis must accurately explain the paper, support its critique with specific evidence, and connect the proposed experiment to the project.

## Fallback

If the paper is inaccessible, ask [PROJECT LEAD] for an accessible author manuscript or an alternative paper covering the same topic.
