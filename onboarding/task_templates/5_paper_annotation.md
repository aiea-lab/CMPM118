# Task 5: Paper Annotation

## Objective

Read and critically annotate one paper that directly supports the project.

## Tasks

- [ ] Read `[PAPER TITLE AND LINK]`.
- [ ] Annotate the research question, motivation, central claim, method, data, evaluation, and limitations.
- [ ] Define unfamiliar terms and note assumptions required by the method.
- [ ] Trace one figure or table from experimental setup to the authors’ conclusion.
- [ ] Identify one claim that is well supported and one that needs more evidence.
- [ ] Connect the paper to the project baseline and propose one follow-up experiment.

## Deliverable

Submit the annotated paper (PDF comments or shared annotation link) and a one-page synthesis. The synthesis must cite page, section, figure, or table locations for its major observations and distinguish the authors’ claims from your interpretation.
