# Task 10: Project Interviews

For your final onboarding task, you will interview with the project(s) that you are interested in joining.

## Interview Instructions

- Review the [AIEA Lab active projects](https://aiea-lab.github.io/research/) and identify the projects that best align with the **topic area you onboarded in**.
- In general, you should interview for projects that match your onboarding topic. For example, if you onboarded in Vision, prioritize Vision projects; if you onboarded in NLP, prioritize NLP projects.
- Schedule a **15-minute interview** with the lead of **each** project you are seriously interested in.
- All interviews must be completed **by the end of Week 10**.
- **Tasks 1–9 must be completed before your interview.** Please do not schedule your interview until your previous onboarding tasks are finished.
- You may interview with more than one project. The goal is to learn about the projects and find a good fit for both you and the project team.
- Come prepared to briefly discuss:
  - what interests you about the project;
  - how the project connects to the topic you onboarded in;
  - relevant experience or skills you have;
  - what you learned during the onboarding tasks;
  - what you would like to learn or work on next quarter; and
  - any questions you have for the project lead.

> **Open projects:** **Neuron Explanations (NeurEx), Hybrid Note, and Olelo** will accept **all students who complete an interview**, regardless of the topic area they onboarded in. You still need to complete the interview so that the team can learn about your interests and determine what part of the project would be a good fit.

## Deliverable and Completing Task 10

Task 10 is complete when:

1. Tasks 1–9 are complete.
2. You have identified the project(s) you are interested in, prioritizing projects that align with the topic area you onboarded in.
3. You have completed a 15-minute interview with **each** project you are considering.
4. If interviewing for NeurEx, you have completed Mattia's mentoring forms.
5. All interviews have been completed by the **end of Week 10**.
6. You have submitted a **Project Report to Canvas**.

### Project Report

You must submit a **Project Report** to Canvas. Your Project Report should consist of **all of your completed Tasks 1–10 combined into one document**.

Please clearly label each task in the report so that your onboarding work can be reviewed in one place.

### Project Assignment

New auditors who successfully complete **Task 10**, including the required interviews and Canvas Project Report, will be assigned to projects **by the end of finals**.

Project assignments will take your onboarding topic, stated interests, and interviews into account.

Students who interview for **NeurEx, Hybrid Note, or Olelo** may be placed on those projects regardless of their onboarding topic.

After project assignments are announced, you will know which project you will begin working with going forward.
